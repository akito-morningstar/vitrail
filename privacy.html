Privacy Policy — Vitrail

Last updated: 2026-11-21 · App: com.akitostudio.vitrail

The game has no accounts and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, or any other personal data. The only data that leaves your device is what Google AdMob needs in order to serve rewarded ads.

Data stored on your device

Only game progress (high scores, Shards, unlocked window themes and glass styles, Journey stars, Daily history) and settings (sound, music, haptics), kept in the app's private storage and never uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded ads only, and only when you tap a button to watch one (to revive a finished game, to get an extra Undo or an extra tray, or to double the Shards you earned). There are no banners and no ads that interrupt gameplay.

To serve these ads, Google AdMob may collect and process your device's advertising ID; IP address, device type, operating system, language and country; ad interaction data (ad viewed, ad clicked); and diagnostic and performance data. This processing is performed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent the first time you open it (Google User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy options in the game. To reset or delete your advertising ID: Android Settings → Google → Ads.

No analytics, no tracking

The game contains no analytics or tracking SDK, has no server of its own, and has no in-app purchases.

Permissions

Internet and network state (to load rewarded ads), vibration (haptic feedback, can be turned off in Settings), and the advertising ID (used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK).

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and does not participate in the Google Play Families programme. The app tells AdMob that its content is not child-directed.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because the game collects no identifying data, there is nothing for the developer to look up, correct, or delete on request. For advertising data processed by Google: withdraw consent under Settings → Ad privacy options (EEA/UK), reset your advertising ID in Android settings, or uninstall the game to delete local data.

Changes

Any future version will be published at this same URL with an updated date.
